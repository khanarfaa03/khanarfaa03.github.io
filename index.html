Community Initiative Internship

Intern, Uday Foundation (NGO)

Project Focus: NGO Compliance & Management | Title: "Health with Dignity" | New Delhi, India

Report Verified (40 Pages)

Core Operational Modules & Compliance Work

1
NGO Foundation & Operational Scope

Founded by Rahul Verma and Tulika Verma following their personal journey caring for their son Uday, the Uday Foundation works at the intersection of healthcare support, emergency food distribution, and child dignity. Operating as a volunteer-driven non-profit, the organization distributes daily hot meals, hygiene kits, and medical aid to vulnerable families outside major government hospitals in New Delhi.

2
Statutory Tax Compliance (Form 10BD & Form 10BE Integration)

I executed statutory tax compliance tasks under Income Tax Department regulations, where non-profit organizations must file an annual statement of donations (**Form 10BD**). I reconciled donor KYC entries (PAN, Aadhar, contact details) across central databases to generate and issue verified **Form 10BE** Section 80G tax exemption certificates to individual donors.

3
In-Kind Inventory & Operational Logistics

I managed physical collection drives and on-ground donor receipt operations. Organized, sorted, and cataloged incoming in-kind contributions including clothes, blankets, toys, medical aid, and hygiene kits for structured relief distribution to families in need.

4
Data Integration, Register Audits & MIS Reporting

I cross-checked manual receipt books and physical registers against master Microsoft Excel spreadsheets to eliminate duplicate entries, verify donor KYC credentials, and generate internal management information system (MIS) reports for NGO leadership.

Event Organiser — Higher Secondary School

Organised and co-managed school events with a team, handling everything from initial planning to day-of operational execution.
