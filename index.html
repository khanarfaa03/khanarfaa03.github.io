1. Executive Overview

During my BBA internship at Uday Foundation, I led operational data logging and compliance initiatives for the "Health with Dignity" project. The initiative focuses on providing food, shelter, clothes, and medical dignity to underprivileged patients and their families traveling to Delhi's government hospitals (such as AIIMS).

2. On-Ground Operations & Gallery

Managed donor intake, cataloged physical donations (clothes, blankets, shoes, ration kits), and organized collection boxes at the Hauz Khas distribution center.

3. Core Technical & Business Responsibilities

  • Database & MIS Architecture: Structured and maintained central donor records in MS Excel to ensure complete audit readiness.
  • Statutory Tax Compliance: Collected and verified donor KYC credentials (PAN / Aadhaar) necessary under Section 80G of the Income Tax Act.
  • Form 10BD & 10BE Automation: Prepared error-free datasets matching government portal requirements for annual Form 10BD filing, enabling efficient distribution of Form 10BE tax-deduction certificates to donors.
  • In-Kind Supply Chain Control: Systematically inventoried physical donations to streamline distribution drives near major government hospitals.

4. Key Results & Impact

  • Achieved 100% data verification accuracy across thousands of donor entries.
  • Significantly reduced the turnaround time for issuing Form 10BE tax certificates to donors.
  • Bridged the gap between physical on-ground charitable collection and digital corporate compliance.
